HOW THE ALLOWANCE WORKS

Advice is advisory. The numbers stay yours.

One analysis run a day
The whole-manuscript reading (critique, roadmap, opening, breakdown, comparables, query letter, readers, market) runs together, once every 24 hours. Results stay open until the next run.
Requests for the rest
Ask your book, fixes and rewrites are counted per request against the daily allowance, 25 on Starter and 75 on Premium.
Scores never move
AI reading is marked as interpretation. It can explain a score or disagree with it; it cannot change one.
What leaves your browser
The diagnostic runs locally. AI features send the relevant passages to the provider when you choose to run them, and only then.
