Example excerpt

The air tasted like ash, and Narubi watched the skyline burn. She had survived worse, but this felt different.

It wasn’t just a city falling.

It was a choice.

Book Intelligence · advisory

The emotion is summarised where the scene could carry it

“This felt different” names the feeling instead of letting the reader find it. The image before it, ash and a burning skyline, is doing the work; consider what Narubi does with her hands, her breath, her attention.

Interpretation, not measurement. Your scores are unchanged.
