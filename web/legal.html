AI-Generated Content

AuthorScrolls uses artificial intelligence (specifically Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's GPT models) to provide manuscript analysis, writing suggestions, and creative feedback.

Important Limitations

  • AI is advisory only. All suggestions are recommendations, not rules. The final creative decisions are always yours.
  • AI can be wrong. Scores, feedback, and suggestions may not always be accurate or appropriate for your genre, audience, or creative intent.
  • AI does not replace human editors. Professional editing by a qualified human editor is recommended before publication.
  • AI-generated text (query letters, blurb drafts, beta reader simulations) should be reviewed and modified before use.

Data Processing

When you use AI features, excerpts of your manuscript (up to 15,000 characters) are sent to AI providers for processing. This data is not stored by the AI providers or used for model training. We use prompt caching to minimize data transmission and costs.

Scores & Metrics

All scores (Overall Score, Engagement, Clarity, etc.) are generated by our proprietary algorithms and AI analysis. They represent one perspective on writing quality and should not be taken as absolute measures of a manuscript's worth or publishability.
